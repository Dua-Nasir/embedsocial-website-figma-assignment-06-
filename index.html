Frequently asked questions

Have a specific question? Check out our Support Center

How does EmbedStories work?

Connect your Instagram Business account, choose the stories you want to show, and copy the embed code onto your website. Your stories then appear as a widget or gallery.

Do I need coding skills to use it?

No. You just copy and paste one snippet of code into your website. It also works with popular website builders and ecommerce platforms.

Can I try it before I pay?

Yes. Every paid plan comes with a 7-day free trial, and there is also a free plan with 1 Instagram Business account that you can use for as long as you like.

Do my new stories update automatically?

Yes. With Auto Update, any new story you post on Instagram appears in your widget without you having to touch your website again.
